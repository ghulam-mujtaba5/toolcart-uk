# Supplier onboarding questionnaire — send before we develop checkout

Subject: UK trade ecommerce account, live inventory feed and direct fulfilment enquiry

We plan to launch a UK online retailer for hardware, tools, fixings and decorating supplies. We have received quote EXP05155 dated 09/10/2026. Please confirm the following in writing:

1. Can our proposed new legal business open a direct wholesale account at quoted pricing? How long are prices valid, and are they fixed/subject to volume discounts?
2. Is **blind dropshipping directly to an individual UK customer** permitted? If not, can we place customer orders for collection or consolidated deliveries to our dispatch hub?
3. Are we allowed to list catalogue products before purchasing, and how should we describe availability to customers?
4. Can you provide CSV/SFTP/API inventory (with update frequency), product list, pack quantities, EANs, wholesale costs, product weights/dimensions and end-of-line flags?
5. Can we reserve stock automatically or by phone/email? How long is reservation held?
6. For direct dispatch: picking/packing charge per order, packaging restrictions, branding, carrier, tracked-delivery cost, surcharges by postcode/weight, daily cut-off, SLAs and peak-season changes?
7. For an order containing 3 different products: is carriage charged per order, per case or per line? Are mixed cartons possible? Minimum order/multiple case restrictions?
8. Who issues customer tracking, when, and can status be delivered by webhook/API/CSV? Are tracking links reliable?
9. What is your returns address/RMA process, defective-item credit procedure, restocking charge, inbound/outbound return postage responsibility and time-to-credit?
10. Warranty claims, damaged goods, recall notifications, manufacturer declarations/CE-UKCA/product safety instructions, PPE certificates, electrical test tool certification and appropriate blade/knife restrictions?
11. Product photos and PDF technical-sheet licence/authorisation for our web store, Merchant Center and marketplaces? Can we edit/crop manufacturer images?
12. Product GTIN ownership, manufacturer codes, accurate physical weights/dimensions, country of origin and VAT rates?
13. When are we charged (prepaid, on dispatch, 7/30-day terms)? Credit limit? Account statement and invoice API?
14. What happens if supplier stock changes or price rises between customer order and your acceptance? Is partial fulfilment supported?
15. Which marketplaces are permitted under your reseller agreement (Amazon/eBay/TikTok)? Any restrictions on branded sales or minimum advertised pricing?
16. Who is responsible for loss in transit; do you insure shipments? What are exclusions for long tools/ladders, liquids, aerosols and dangerous goods?
17. Are you willing to process 3–5 UK sample transactions and a failed/returned sample before our public launch?

We need product feed samples, dispatch pricing sheet, commercial account terms and proof that direct-to-consumer fulfilment is supported. A wholesale quote alone does not confirm this.

Internal owner follow-up: Business structure, partnership shares, ownership of domain/code/catalogue, VAT/accountant, company bank and Stripe KYC must be resolved separately.
