# Evidence-based competitor UX and content benchmark
Research accessed 12 October 2026. Method: first-party live merchant site pages and authoritative UX guidance. **This is public webpage/document inspection, not a comprehensive usability test, automated crawl or systematic real-user measurement.** Do not claim we measured page speeds, conversion rates, backlinks or Google keyword volumes.

## UX evidence beyond competitors
Baymard, 9 Sep 2025: https://baymard.com/research-articles/current-state-product-list-and-filtering
- 58% desktop / 78% mobile in benchmark were mediocre-or-worse on product list UX.
- Recommend category-relevant filters, applied-filter summaries, comparison-friendly SKU cards, visible unit pricing for pack sizes, meaningful thumbnails and sorting. **These are general industry UX findings, not a measured ToolCart uplift.**
Baymard, 2026: https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-filter-ui
- Mobile filters generally work better with an explicit "Show X results" action after selecting filters; include essential price, brand, size etc and technical category-specific facets.
Baymard listings: https://baymard.com/research-articles/product-listing-information
- Show enough key product attributes to avoid forcing every shopper to open a product page.

## Competition conclusion
Incumbents are not universally poor: Screwfix has sophisticated inventory/customer journeys, Toolstation broad catalogue/navigation, Axminster authoritative technical guidance. **Potential wedge:** verified compatibility and multi-product project baskets for problems novice buyers cannot express in SKU terms; be honest about supplier stock/delivery and be faster on targeted tasks, not necessarily every UX dimension.

## Why not clone websites verbatim?
Use design pattern inspiration only. Site scraping and lifting photos/product descriptions/logo/HTML can violate rights and damage distinctiveness. Use authorised supplier assets and independently written technical details; legal review for permissions/licences where needed.
