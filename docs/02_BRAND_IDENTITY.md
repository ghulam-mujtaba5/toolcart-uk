# ToolCart V3 — Final visual colour authority

**Brand name is provisional (UK trademark and domain status NOT cleared).** Other baseline supplier-first, legal and engineering constraints stay in force.

## Brand purpose
UK-first online DIY/hardware retailer, both consumer and trade. Emphasise reliable catalogue, honest stock/delivery, guided product selection and authentic helpfulness. The positioning **The right tools. The right advice.** is proposed copy, not a factual claim of professional safety advice.

Main purchase CTA: cobalt with white text (7.21:1). Lime accents: Ink text (12.66:1); lime action on white requires a dark 2px outline. **Never use white text on lime** (1.25:1). Do not use Steel Grey as ordinary body text on off-white or pale-blue surfaces (fails AA 4.5:1).

## Prohibited and retired
Copper `#B84C32` is removed; no purple, violet, lavender, teal-as-brand, AI-neon gradients or invented extra hues. Original Steel Grey stays approved only for appropriate backgrounds. Functional status colours are necessary exceptions, never decorative palette alternatives.

## Typography
Keep **Manrope** headings (600–800) and **IBM Plex Sans** body and interface (400–700). Product title, prices and dimensions must be exceptionally readable. Don't include font binary files in handoff package.

## Usage
- Product cards: white on Workshop White, subdued decorative border, verified manufacturer/supplier image.
- Search/CTA: cobalt primary. Lime outline variant only when purpose and contrast are clear.
- AI: Cloud Blue panel, Ink text and cobalt primary action; no competing assistant brand.
- Logo: must work in Ink on white, pure white on Ink, and mono print.
- Print: run physical proofs and find print colour equivalents before manufacturing. HEX specifies screen colours only.
