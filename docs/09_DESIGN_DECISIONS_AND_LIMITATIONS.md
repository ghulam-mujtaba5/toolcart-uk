# Brand and development decisions — V3

## Brand changes approved for design implementation
- Single brand system: Ink + Cobalt + Action Lime + neutral whites/blues.
- No Copper, purple/violet/lavender or teal brand identity.
- Primary CTA is Cobalt + White, accent is Action Lime + Ink, outlined if used as a functional control against white.
- Accessible secondary text on off-white/pale blue is `#596B70` (the previous `#66767A` fails in these settings).
- Type is Manrope + IBM Plex Sans.
- AI chat is Cloud Blue and Cobalt, same brand, not an independent neon identity.
- `design/tokens.css` and `design/tokens.json` supersede all previous palette values.

## No change to business/development safety gates
ToolCart company/domain/trademark still pending verification. Supplier's 9 October 2026 quotation establishes wholesale cost only, not fulfilment SLA, rights to photographs or live availability. Do not enable production checkout until legal merchant, VAT, payments, real stock, safety and delivery have been approved.

## Evidence vs design judgment
Exact WCAG 2.2 ratios are computed programmatically in `qa/contrast_matrix.csv`; Pinterest 2026 trend shades are sourced. Everything about general UK colour preference, trust or conversion is an inference to be tested, not a verified finding.
