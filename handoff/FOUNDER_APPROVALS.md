# Founder decisions needed (blocking vs non-blocking)

## Blocking LIVE SALES
- [ ] Domain purchase + brand trademark clearance, Companies House check, trading name/entity ownership
- [ ] Legal seller of record, UK merchant eligibility, VAT/accounting treatment, UK business bank & payment provider verification
- [ ] Supplier signed agreement: dropship/stock feed/returns, supplier delivery charges, dispatch cut-off, direct-to-customer and neutral packaging
- [ ] Confirm product images rights, restricted/bladed goods sale/age verification, safety docs, any hazardous shipping categories
- [ ] Set final GBP VAT-inclusive consumer prices, delivery rules, withdrawal/returns/privacy/terms pages
- [ ] Test orders, carrier label/tracking, refund path, damaged/lost parcel escalations

## Non-blocking STAGING
- [ ] Compare two selected colours with 5 DIY + 5 trade users; record customer feedback (not invented conversion lift)
- [ ] Confirm preferred customer tagline and licensed Google Fonts use
- [ ] Verify which supplier CSV/API can be accessed and mapping cadence
- [ ] Agree founders' domain/code/customer-data ownership in writing
