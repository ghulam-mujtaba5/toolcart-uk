# United Shop — separate e-commerce visuals · Round 1

This is a reusable starter visual asset pack for a UK trade & DIY hardware store, based on the product categories on the live ToolCart staging prototype. The **United Shop** name is a *working direction* from the earlier conversation; the staging site still uses ToolCart.

## Folders

01_Brand — editable SVG logos, raster logo, favicon, primary + staging name choices.
02_Hero_Banners — separately exported clean, branded, desktop, mobile, dark variations.
03_Category_Images — SIX separate product-category images with plain and labelled alternatives.
04_Promo_Banners — three separate promotional images: decorating, measuring, safety.
05_Social_Marketing — two square posts, one vertical story, one email header.
06_Illustrations — independent original editable SVG drawings (+ PNG).
07_Site_Icons — four lightweight SVG+PNG value-proposition icons.
08_Original_Artwork — six individual full-resolution original AI-generated imagery files.
09_Developer_Guide — practical integration notes, asset manifest, colour tokens.

## Important

These assets are inspired by modern UK hardware-commerce layouts but are **newly created**, not exact copies or downloaded competitor photos. No rights to competitor branding are implied.

The images are illustrative marketing artwork, not product photos of listed SKUs or verified inventory. Before use, confirm the final brand/domain, all copy and claims, your image optimisation settings, and supplier/product requirements.

No combined image sheet is included: every image is a standalone export. The full set can be extended in a second round.
